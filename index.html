I’m a master’s student in the MVA program (Mathematics, Vision, Learning) at ENS Paris-Saclay, which I follow in parallel with the research track of the engineering program at CentraleSupélec.

My work sits where probability meets deep learning: flow matching, optimal transport and the geometry of learned representations. At Mila, in the lab of Prof. Danilo Bzdok, I built a conditional flow matching model that turns a single molecular embedding back into a complete 3D molecule, as part of a project linking deep molecular representations to their neuroscience effects.

Before Mila, I spent six months at Edo Theory drawing an analogy between percolation and cryptocurrency limit order books, and building deep learning strategies for mid-frequency trading. I also deployed a probabilistic sales forecasting system for a startup that still runs in production. I’m currently working on JEPA world models that plan compiler optimizations in latent space.

Where I want to go next is audio. Sound is a signal where the tools I care about, flow matching, diffusion and optimal transport, meet hard constraints of time and structure, and models like Kyutai’s Moshi show how far real time generation can go.

I come from Bordeaux, I’ve played tennis for fifteen years and guitar for seven, and I served as vice president of the CentraleSupélec Forum.

Interests

AI for audio first: generative models that listen, speak and make music, in the spirit of Kyutai’s Moshi: speech and music generation, neural audio codecs, full duplex dialogue in real time.

Around it, the mathematics and models I keep coming back to.

  • Generative models

    How models learn high dimensional distributions, and how to sample from them well.

  • Flow matching and diffusion

    Probability paths, couplings, guidance, and what makes sampling fast.

  • Optimal transport

    Wasserstein geometry, entropic regularization, and transport as a lens on generative modeling.

  • Percolation

    First passage percolation and random growth, a thread from my work on order books.

  • Graph neural networks

    Equivariant message passing for molecules and other structured data.

  • World models

    Predictive latent models such as JEPA, and planning inside them.

Selected work

MaceMolFlow

One vector in. One 3D molecule out.

A conditional flow matching decoder that rebuilds a full molecule, with atoms, bonds, formal charges and 3D coordinates, from a single pooled MACE-OFF embedding. I built it on FLOWR, warm-started from its pretrained weights, during my research internship at Mila with Prof. Danilo Bzdok.

View on GitHub

Optimal transport, by hand

My reimplementation of the MVA course by Gabriel Peyré and Julie Delon, extended.

McCann interpolation, entropic barycenters, semi discrete transport and flow matching couplings. Plus a few experiments of my own, like a glass slab whose height is a Brenier potential, so that sunlight passing through it draws an image.
